HERITAGE MEETS LASER PRECISION

The Art of Multi-Layered Relief Woodcraft

Each wall sculpture begins with sustainably sourced Russian birch plywood and solid walnut sheets, calibrated to millimeter tolerances. Our artisans stack between 6 to 9 distinct architectural tiers, hand-gluing each layer to create breathtaking 3D shadows and visual depth that changes with room light.
